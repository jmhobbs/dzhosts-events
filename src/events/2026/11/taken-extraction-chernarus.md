Two teams: Kidnappers and The Rescuers.

Chernarus has become the hunting ground for a ruthless kidnapping operation. A young person has been abducted and is being held captive inside a heavily guarded warehouse, somewhere within the industrial sprawl of Chernarus. Their captors believe they have time on their side. They believe nobody knows where they are. And they believe nobody is coming for them.

They're wrong.

Following a desperate search for information, the location has finally been uncovered. Now, an operation involving the FBI and the person's father is being assembled. With law enforcement providing support and a father driven by pure determination, the rescue team must locate the warehouse, breach the kidnappers' defences, and extract the captive alive.

But this won't be a simple rescue. The kidnappers are armed, organised, and prepared to defend their prize. Every decision matters, every second counts, and one wrong move could mean the difference between a successful extraction and a mission ending in disaster.
