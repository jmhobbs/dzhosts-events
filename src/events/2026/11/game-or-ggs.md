Deal or No Deal, remastered for DayZ. 11 players face 1 banker. The highest deal wins a Badlands Steam key for them or their community.

To apply, DM Gazz1494 on Discord.

### How It Works

- A wheel picks the order of players during the event.
- On your turn, choose a teddy bear as your own.
- Select the remaining teddy bears in your chosen order to reveal the cash amounts removed from the $ board. The sequence is 5-3-3-3-3-3-3.
- After each stage, the banker makes a cash offer. Take the deal or play on.
- If you complete every sequence, two teddy bears are left. Keep your original bear or swap it before the amount is revealed.
- The player with the highest cash amount at the end of everyone's turn wins a Badlands Steam key for them or their community.

Each turn should take around 15 minutes, so the event should run around 3 hours. Players will not be rushed, to build suspense with each decision. The host promises it will not last longer than 24 hours.

### Before the Event

- Players can spawn into the server from 17:00 GMT to pick an outfit from the clothes store.
- Be in the studio green room by 17:45 GMT, ready to take up your positions on the set.
- Server details are posted in the host's Discord 1 day before the event.
