This is a collaboration between Hallowqueenttv & TheArriana

Chernarus is dead. Survival is all that remains.

Teams of four women will begin on the coast, fighting their way across the wasteland toward the final extraction point where they fight a horde before boarding a helicopter for extraction.

Scavenge. Fight. Survive.

Only one team leaves.

This event is to raise money for the Malala Fund, we ask that all participants will share this link with there communities if they have one. During the event viewers will have the option to donate and unlock rewards for our players if milestones are reached, a graphic will be posted in the discord to showcase what these options will be.
