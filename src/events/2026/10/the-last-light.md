Two teams. One objective. How the night unfolds is up to you!

### Schedule

- 20:00 UK: Meet at Marek Barq Hydroelectric Plant. Teams are picked here.
- 20:30 UK: The target location is announced. Both teams depart under a temporary ceasefire so they can separate. Departure is at 20:30 sharp and will not be delayed.
- 21:00 UK: The ceasefire ends and hostilities are permitted. Teams push toward the objective and try to secure it.
- 00:00 UK: The server restart ends the event.

### Win Conditions

- At least one team member must be in the objective room, with no opposing team members inside.
- No team in the objective: no winner.
- Both teams in the objective, regardless of numbers: no winner.

### Running Late?

If you arrive after the teams have departed, you'll need to make your own way into the action. Find one of the groups and ask if they'll take you in. That is up to them, and neither team will wait or change its plans.

Death doesn't have to end your night. If you die, you may rejoin your original team. Getting back to the action and finding your teammates is up to you.

### Your Story, Your Decisions

Come ready to adapt, work together, and make your own decisions. There are no guarantees, and not everything will go to plan.

When the light fades, what will remain?
