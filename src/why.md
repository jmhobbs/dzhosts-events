# Why does this site exist?

On October 4th, 2026, [iRoadie announced](https://discord.com/channels/1073189229842145292/1084432928207016088/1556230734535069736) that the `Independent DayZ Event Calendar` would be put on hold.  Since I greatly appreciate the work iRoadie did, and all the great events the community puts on, I decided to make this site to carry the torch a while and help people keep track of events moving forward.

This site is not meant to be forever. I hope someday iRoadie will be back at it, and when that day comes this site won't be needed anymore.


## Open Everything

This site is <a href="https://github.com/jmhobbs/dzhosts-events">open source</a>, and open to events and changes.  No secret sauce, no hidden data, this exists for the community, and belongs to the community.
